🇮🇹 ItalyDocsHelper Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 7, 2026

🔒 In short: Your form answers and the PDFs you create are saved only on your device. We have no server that stores your documents. A few features send text to outside services so they can work; this policy lists each one, what is sent and how to avoid it.

1. Introduction

ItalyDocsHelper helps people in Italy who do not speak Italian understand and prepare Italian documents. It is an independent app, not affiliated with the Italian government or any public body. It produces drafts only, which you print, sign and submit yourself through official channels.

We process personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (Regulation (EU) 2016/679). This policy explains what stays on your device, what is sent to third-party services and why, and what we keep.

2. Who We Are (Data Controller)

SADEVELOPERS (independent developer)
Email: swdeveloper.sa@gmail.com

3. What Stays On Your Device

  • Your form answers (for example name, date of birth, address, codice fiscale, passport and permit details) for the Permesso di Soggiorno kit and the other forms.
  • The PDFs you generate. They are saved in the app's storage on your device and stay there until you delete them or uninstall the app. On iPhone they also appear in the Files app.
  • Text recognition (OCR) of photos you take or import, done with Google ML Kit models that run on the device. Photos are not uploaded for OCR.
  • Your settings, the assistant chat history and its daily usage counter.

We do not upload, store or back up your documents, scans, photos, form answers or PDFs on any server we control. They are shared only when you choose to share them, for example through the system share sheet.

4. What Is Sent To Other Services, And Why

Some features cannot work on the device alone. When you use one of them, the data below is sent over an encrypted connection.

FeatureWhat is sentTo whom
AI Assistant (Android and iPhone)The question you type and the last messages of that chatOpenRouter, Inc. (USA), which passes it to an AI model provider
Translate Document (Android only)The recognised text of the document, not the photoGoogle (Gemini, with Google Translate as backup)
Scan Form / ID or passport scan (Android only)The recognised text, not the photoGoogle Gemini
Usage statistics and crash reportsAnonymous events and crash data (see section 6)Google Firebase

a. The AI Assistant

  • The app asks for your permission first. Before your first question is sent, a dialog explains what is sent and to whom. Nothing is sent unless you tap Allow, and you can take the permission back at any time with the shield button on the assistant screen.
  • Before sending, the app removes things that look like a codice fiscale, a document number, a phone number or an e-mail address. You should still not type personal data into the assistant.
  • The assistant uses free AI models. Some of their providers may keep what you type to improve their models, as described in OpenRouter's privacy policy.
  • Your form answers and PDFs are never sent to the assistant.
  • To check its answers, the app also downloads public pages from official websites (for example portaleimmigrazione.it, poliziadistato.it, agenziaentrate.gov.it). Those requests contain no information about you.

b. Translation and scanning (Android only)

The recognised text can contain personal data such as names, dates of birth and document numbers. It is used only to return the result to you and is not stored by us. Google processes it as our service provider under its API terms. If you prefer not to send text to Google, fill the form by hand instead of using these features. These two features are not available in the iPhone version.

Legal basis: performance of the service you request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR); for the AI Assistant, your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR).

5. Accounts And Purchases

The current version has no sign-up and no purchases: we do not collect your name or e-mail address, and we never see payment details.

If you signed in with an earlier version, your name and e-mail address are kept in Firebase (Google Cloud) until you delete your account from Profile > Delete account, or ask us by e-mail. If accounts or paid features return in a later version, this policy will be updated first.

6. Analytics And Crash Reports

We use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to see which features are used and to fix crashes (for example "form opened", "PDF exported"). These events contain no document content and nothing you type into forms. They may include device model, OS version and a random installation identifier. We do not use them for advertising and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

7. Data Retention

  • Form answers and PDFs: kept on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Never stored by us.
  • Assistant questions: not stored by us. The chat history stays on your device and you can clear it on the assistant screen. Retention by OpenRouter and the model providers follows their own policies.
  • Text sent to Google for translation or scanning: not retained by us; returned to you and discarded.
  • Analytics: kept by Firebase for up to 14 months.

8. Sharing And International Transfers

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers. The only recipients are the service providers named in section 4. OpenRouter and Google may process data on servers outside the European Union, including in the United States, under their own GDPR safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses).

9. Your Data Protection Rights Under GDPR

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time.

  • Data on your device: you control it. Edit or delete it in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything.
  • Assistant consent: withdraw it with the shield button on the assistant screen.
  • Anything else: e-mail us at swdeveloper.sa@gmail.com. Since we do not store your documents, there is no document data for us to provide or delete.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. In Italy this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

10. Camera And Photo Permissions

Where the app asks for the camera or your photos, it is only to photograph or pick a document you choose. Text recognition runs on the device and the photo is not uploaded. The app does not read your photo library in the background.

11. Data Security

Your data is stored in the app's private storage, protected by your device's own security. All connections to the services in section 4 are encrypted (HTTPS).

12. Children's Privacy

ItalyDocsHelper is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

13. Changes To This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy. The new version is posted on this page with a new effective date, and the in-app Privacy Policy is updated with it.
